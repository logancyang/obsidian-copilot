# Replies

How a reply appears in a conversation: streaming text, reasoning, tool and activity cards, subagents, and tasks.

## Sub-features

- reply-stream: the reply's text appears as the agent writes it.
- reply-tables: streamed Markdown tables keep their headers and body columns aligned; wide tables scroll horizontally inside the reply.
- reply-duration: `Worked for <time>`, counting up while the response runs.
- reply-reasoning: `Reasoning...` while the agent thinks, then `Thought for <time>`; expand it to read the reasoning.
- reply-activity: the agent's tool calls collapse into a one-line summary, such as `Ran 2 commands, read 1 file`; expand it to see each step.
- reply-earlier-messages: a long chat opens at its latest messages; scrolling up loads earlier messages above them without moving what is on screen, and expanded activity stays expanded.
- reply-plan: the agent's `Plan` checklist, with each step's progress.
- reply-connector-tools: a tool call to a connected service is named `<service> · <tool>`, such as `Notion · Search`, with every agent; OpenCode calls connected tools directly, with a separate named step for each call. Native OpenCode code execution still shows `Execute`; older saved wrapped calls keep their recorded labels.
- reply-subagents: a subagent's card shows its type and task, such as `general-purpose · Count lines in notes.md`, with a spinner while it runs and a check when it is done. With Claude, open it to see its prompt, its steps, and its result, then `Back to chat`; with other agents it expands in place, with its `Prompt`.
- reply-tasks: a background command shows as a card with a spinner, then a check; open it to see the command and its output, then `Back to chat`.
- reply-task-follow-up: when a background task or subagent finishes, the agent replies again on its own.
- reply-compaction: `Compacting conversation…`, then `Context compacted` with the token count before and after; when the context fills up, `Context is full, compacting…`, then `Context compacted automatically`.
- reply-files-changed: a response that edited notes ends with its [turn diffs](turn-diffs.md).
- reply-remembered: in a chat with a teammate, a rule the teammate saves to its memory shows as a card, in the Miyo search card's style, in place of its save step: `<name> remembered` with the saved sentence in quotes on one line, cut with an ellipsis when long. Clicking the card opens it to the full sentence and a `MEMORY` row (with the teammate's folder) that opens the teammate's `MEMORY.md` at that rule; the row's tooltip is the file's path. There is no Undo, and a save adds nothing to the reply's changed files. A rule saved by a [teammate you asked](asking-a-teammate.md) shows the same card, with that teammate's name, under the `Message from` card of the reply that brings its answer back.
- reply-replaced-rule: when a saved rule replaces an older one, the card reads `<name> replaced a rule`; opened, it shows the old rule struck through above the new one.
- reply-rule-contradiction: when a saved rule goes against the teammate's instruction, the card shows, without being opened, a warning under its header: `Goes against what <name> handles: “<sentence>” This rule wins for now.` with `Open instruction`, which opens the teammate's `instructions.md`.
- reply-open-instruction: asked to change its instruction, a teammate changes nothing and replies with an `Open instruction` link to its `instructions.md`.
- reply-agent-draft: with a Supporter license, asked to make a new teammate, a teammate or a Copilot chat shows a `New teammate draft` card under its reply with the draft's name, title, and instruction (cut to three lines, with `Show more`), and `Not now` and `Review and create…`. The card has no Create: `Review and create…` opens the [New teammate dialog](new-teammate-dialog.md) filled in. Asking for a change in the chat replaces the card with the new draft; a chat has at most one. `Not now` removes the card, and so does creating the teammate from it; closing the dialog without creating leaves the card as it was. A card still in the chat comes back as it was when the chat is reopened. If the teammate you are chatting with or another teammate already handles the job, the reply names it and asks before drafting. Without a Supporter license, no chat shows a draft card.
- reply-earlier-chats: asked what you worked on, discussed or decided before, a teammate lists its own earlier chats, archived ones and those in an archived project included, newest first, by date and title (its step reads `obsidian-copilot · List chats`), opens a chat's note to say what was decided, and says when something was never discussed. A chat that was not saved as a note is known by its title and date only. It never answers from another teammate's chat or a Copilot chat. After its own chats it lists up to 10 conversations another teammate or Copilot [`@`-asked it into](asking-a-teammate.md), newest first: the date, who asked, the asking chat's title, how many times it was asked there, what it was first asked, and its latest answer or that it was stopped or didn't finish. It knows nothing else of the chat that asked. A Copilot chat doesn't list earlier chats.
- reply-actions: one row with `Copy` and `Insert / Replace at cursor` at the end of each finished response, which is everything the agent says between two of your messages: its replies, its steps, and the `Message from` cards of teammates it asked. `Copy` and `Insert` take the text of every reply in the response, in order, without the messages of teammates it asked. The row is dimmed and brightens while the pointer is on the response or keyboard focus is inside it. While a reply is being written its response has no row; when the agent says more without a new message from you, the row moves to the new end. The newest response's row starts with `Worked for` and the agent's own replies in the response added up.
- message-time: a short clock time such as `2:02 PM` (`14:02` in a 24-hour locale), with the full date and time as its tooltip. An older response shows its time at the left of its row while the pointer is on it. Your message shows nothing at rest; pointing at it or focusing it floats its time and `Copy` in a small bar at the bubble's lower right, over the gap below, and nothing moves.
- day-labels: a label above the first message of each day: `Today`, `Yesterday`, the weekday for the six days before that, then the date such as `Nov 10`, with the year when it is not this year. A chat that began today has none; the labels update when midnight passes with the chat open.
- reply-continue: `Continue from here in a new chat` in a finished response's row, while the chat is not replying. It saves the chat as a note, saves the conversation up to the end of that response as a new chat note, and opens that note in a new tab with the same title, agent, model, and project. The new chat shows the conversation as plain messages, with their attached notes, web tabs and images. Until a message is sent there, picking another agent's model in `Model · effort` moves the chat, with those messages and its note, to that agent. The first message sent there tells the agent that conversation, with the tools each reply used and where earlier images are saved, and names the original chat's note (and the notes of any chats it was continued from) as notes it must not read, because they hold later messages. Reopening the new chat later shows those messages, then the new messages with their tool steps.

## How to get to it

Send a message from the [chat input](chat-input.md). The reply shows below the message in the chat. Running background tasks are also listed under `N tasks running` in the [chat controls](chat-controls.md).

## Driving it

- Stream and duration: send a short question. `Worked for <time>` counts up, the reply's text appears, then the time stops.
- Tables: ask for a Markdown table with short headers and long cell values. During streaming and after completion, headers align with their columns. In a narrow pane, a wide table scrolls horizontally without widening the reply.
- Reasoning: ask a question the agent thinks about. `Reasoning...` shows, then `Thought for <time>`; expand it to read the reasoning.
- Activity: ask the agent to run two shell commands. A summary such as `Ran 2 commands` shows; expand it to list each command.
- Earlier messages: reopen a chat with more than 10 messages from `Recent chats`. Only the latest messages show. Expand an activity summary, then scroll up: earlier messages appear above while the visible text stays in place. Scroll back down: the summary is still expanded.
- Plan: ask an agent with a plan tool to plan two steps and follow them. A `Plan` checklist shows each step's progress.
- Connector tool: with Notion connected, ask the agent to search Notion. The step reads `Notion · Search`, not the tool's internal name.
- OpenCode connector batch: ask OpenCode to use a connected service twice. Each call appears as its own named step, without an Execute prefix. A native resource-discovery or tool-search script can still appear as Execute.
- Subagent: ask Claude to start a subagent that reads a note. A card such as `general-purpose · Get first sentence of <note>` shows a spinner, then a check. Open it: the subagent's prompt, its steps, and its answer show. `Back to chat` returns to the conversation.
- Background task: ask Claude to run `ping -c 15 127.0.0.1` as a background command. Its card shows a spinner and the chat controls show `1 task running`. Open it: `Background command`, the command, and its output show.
- Follow-up: wait for the subagent or background command to finish. The agent posts a new reply about the result without a new message.
- Compaction: send `/compact`. `Compacting conversation…` shows, then `Context compacted · 41k → 12k tokens` (the numbers vary).
- Files changed: ask the agent to edit a note. The reply ends with the changed file and its added and removed lines.
- Remembered (needs a Supporter license): in a chat with a teammate in `Default` mode, send `Remember this: launch videos lead with user value, not the feature list.` No permission card shows; `<name> remembered` shows the sentence, and the teammate's `MEMORY.md` now ends with it and a dated context line. Click the card, then its `MEMORY` row: `MEMORY.md` opens at that rule. Send `The launch is on Dec 2.`: nothing is saved and no `remembered` card shows.
- Replaced a rule (same license): send `Remember: summarize as bullet points.`, then `Stop with the bullets. Write summaries as short paragraphs.` The card reads `<name> replaced a rule`; opened, it shows the old rule struck through; `MEMORY.md` keeps only the new rule, its context line starting `Replaces`.
- Rule against the instruction (same license): give a teammate whose instruction says `File quarterly estimated taxes.` the message `From now on you don't need to handle taxes. Leo does that.` The card shows the warning quoting that sentence; `Open instruction` opens the teammate's `instructions.md`.
- Open instruction (same license): send `Change your instruction: you don't handle tax filing anymore.` The reply says only you can change it and links `Open instruction`; neither `instructions.md` nor `MEMORY.md` changes.
- Teammate draft (same license): in a chat with a teammate, send `Make me a teammate for family logistics: school pickups, swim class, camp sign-ups.` A `New teammate draft` card shows a name, title, and instruction with `Not now` and `Review and create…`, and nothing new is in the teammates folder. Send `Also have it remind me of camp deadlines a week ahead.`: the card shows the new draft, and no earlier card remains. Choose `Review and create…` and press Esc: the card is still there. Choose it again and `Create`: back in the drafting chat, the card is gone and stays away after the chat is reopened. On another draft, choose `Not now`: the card goes away, and stays away after the chat is reopened. Ask for a teammate that an existing teammate already covers: the reply names it and asks before drafting. Ask it to `just create it`: it shows a card instead, and the teammates folder is unchanged. In a Copilot chat, send `Create a new teammate called Rex that tracks my reading list.`: the card shows, and `Review and create…` opens the dialog without a `Drafted by` line. Without a Supporter license, the same message shows no card.
- Earlier chats (same license): with a teammate that has a few earlier chats, start a new chat with it and send `What have we been working on lately?` The reply names its recent chats with their dates and titles, matching its `Recent chats` plus its archived chats and its chats in archived projects. Send `What did we decide about <topic of one of them>?`: it answers from that chat. Ask about a topic only another teammate discussed: it says you haven't discussed it. From Coco's chat, send `@Maya is $8k right for the launch?`, then a follow-up `@Maya` question; in a new chat with Maya, send `What have we talked about?`: she says Coco asked her, names the topic and gives her answer, and Recent chats and her profile show no new chat. Delete Coco's chat and ask her again in a new chat: she doesn't mention it. Ask for something unrelated, such as a release note: no `List chats` step shows. In a Copilot chat, ask what you have been working on: it lists no earlier chats.
- One row per response: in a chat with a teammate, ask it to have another teammate check something with `@<name>`. While the teammate you asked works, a dimmed row with `Worked for` sits under the first reply. After the `Message from` card and the second reply, there is one row, at the end, and `Worked for` adds up both replies. Point at the response: the row brightens. Point at your message: its time and `Copy` float at the bubble's lower right, and nothing moves. Send another message: the earlier response keeps its row and shows its time while pointed at. Press Tab through the chat: the rows and your messages' bars show while their buttons have focus. Hover a time: the full date and time show.
- Day labels: reopen a chat with messages from yesterday and today from `Recent chats`. `Yesterday` shows above its first message and `Today` above the first message sent today.
- Copy: choose `Copy` in a finished response's row. The text of every reply in the response is on the clipboard, without the messages of teammates it asked.
- Insert: place the cursor in a note and choose `Insert / Replace at cursor` in a finished response's row. The response's text is inserted at the cursor.
- Continue: in a chat of three exchanges, where the first message states a codeword and the second asks the agent to read a note, choose `Continue from here in a new chat` in the second response's row. A new tab shows the first two exchanges without tool steps and without the third; the original tab is unchanged and `Open chat note` is offered for it. Ask for the codeword, which note was read, and the third exchange's fact: the agent answers the first two and does not know the third, without opening the original chat's note. Close and reopen the new chat from Recent chats: the two carried exchanges show, then the new response with its tool steps.

## Gotchas

- A teammate saves a rule without a permission card, and each save adds or replaces one rule in its own `MEMORY.md`, leaving the others as they were. An edit of any file, `MEMORY.md` included, asks as usual and shows in the changed files. In `Plan` mode it saves nothing and says it can't save a rule in this mode. A Copilot chat never shows `remembered` cards.
- Saved rules apply from the teammate's next chat; an open chat keeps the memory it started with.
- A teammate recalls at most its 20 most recent chats, and only by title, date and note: it does not search what earlier chats said. Archived chats and the chats of an archived project are recalled like any other. A conversation a teammate was `@`-asked into is not one of its earlier chats: it is recalled only from what it was first asked and its latest answer, each cut to about 300 characters and kept on this device, the 100 most recent across all teammates. Deleting the chat that asked, or the teammate that was asked, forgets it.

- `Continue from here in a new chat` saves both chats as notes even when chats are not saved automatically, so `Open chat note` appears in the [chat controls](chat-controls.md) of both, and the new chat is listed in Recent chats before anything is sent in it.
- A finished background task or subagent starts a new agent reply on its own, so a chat can be running with no message from the user. That reply joins the response before it, and the response's row moves to its end.
