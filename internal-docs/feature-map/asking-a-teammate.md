# Asking a teammate

Asking a teammate from a chat with `@`: the teammate you asked works in the background, and its reply comes back to the chat. Below, Coco is the teammate you are chatting with and Maya is the teammate you asked; in a Copilot chat, Copilot takes Coco's part.

## Sub-features

- mention-ask: in a teammate's chat or a Copilot chat, a message that `@`-mentions a teammate by name, such as `@Maya what do you think of this plan?`, hands Maya the request.
- mention-one-line: Coco answers in one line, such as "Maya's reading it. I'll bring back what she says.", and its reply ends. The chat input is free while Maya works.
- mention-view: choosing an ask card, a `Message from` card, or Maya's row in the [chat controls](chat-controls.md)' running list opens Coco's conversation with Maya in place of the chat, read-only; it slides in from the right, and stays still when the system asks for reduced motion. One row heads it: Back, Coco's face and name (Copilot's icon in a Copilot chat), a double arrow, and Maya's face and name; it takes the place of the chat's own header. What Coco sent comes first as a user's message; an answer Coco passed on is marked `Coco passed on your answer`. Below come Maya's steps, the files she changed, and her replies, scrolled to the latest; work she did after being asked again starts below a `Continued at 10:21 AM` divider, its time written as message times are, with the full date and time as its tooltip. While she waits on the user, a banner says so and `Answer there` returns to the chat. There is no chat input: `You can only watch here. To tell Leo something, tell Coco.` Back or Escape returns to the chat.
- mention-ask-card: in Coco's reply, asking a teammate is one card from the moment Coco starts writing to her: `Asking` with Maya's face and name and `…` until the message is sent, then `Messaged` with the face and name. Until Coco has named who it asks, the card reads `Asking …` with a blank face; the face and name then fill in on the same card. It shows no message text; the name is brighter than the label. A message to a teammate that is already working reads `Messaged` too. The other things Coco does with Maya are one line each with her face: `Checked Maya's progress`, `Passed your answer to Maya` and `Stopped Maya`, with nothing to open. A call that failed or named no teammate the chat can ask shows its ordinary row once it ends.
- mention-several: one message can mention several teammates, such as `@Maya @Leo does the $8k launch budget hold up?`. Each works on its own and sees nothing of the other's work; each ask gets its own `Messaged` card, in the order Coco asked. Coco passes each reply on as it comes back, without waiting for the others, and says who is still working; when the last one is back it can put the replies together.
- mention-keep-chatting: messages sent while Maya works get their own answers.
- mention-reply-back: when Maya is done, her reply comes back in a new reply at the end of the chat that passes it on, keeping her position as strong as it was, then adding Coco's own view.
- mention-reply-card: that reply starts with a card like the ask card: `Message from` with Maya's face and name. It shows none of her words in the chat; choosing it opens the conversation. A Files changed card for the files she changed follows, each row opening its diff, then Coco's own words. Maya's question reads `Message from` too. Work that ended with nothing to pass on reads `Maya stopped` or `Maya didn't finish` on the same card. The same shows in a Copilot chat.
- mention-continue: mentioning the same teammate again in the same chat continues its conversation, so it remembers the earlier exchange.
- mention-redirect: a new instruction for a teammate that is still working, such as "Tell Maya to skip the outro.", reaches her in the same conversation. Coco says it passed it on; on Codex and OpenCode models Maya gets it after her current step.
- mention-question: when Maya can't go on without the user, her question comes back in a reply that starts with her words, and Coco asks it in its own words. The user answers in the chat input as usual; Coco passes the answer on and Maya carries on in the same conversation. Until the user answers, Maya waits; asking about something else meanwhile gets its own answer.
- mention-progress: asking Coco how Maya is doing, such as "How's Maya doing?", gets an answer from her progress so far: how long she has worked, what she has written and her latest steps, or the question she is waiting on. Maya isn't interrupted.
- mention-permission: in a mode that asks before changes, Maya's request shows in this chat as a [permission card](permission-requests.md) with her face and name. The answer goes straight back to her; Coco does not reply to it. In `Plan` mode Maya changes nothing and says what she would have changed.
- mention-didnt-finish: when Maya hits an error or her agent's process ends, Coco says she didn't finish, why, and how far she got. There is no Retry: telling Coco to send her back, such as "Send Maya back to it.", continues the same conversation.
- mention-resume: mentioning a teammate again after Obsidian restarts continues the conversation it had in that chat, so it remembers the earlier exchange. This holds for a chat with a note and for one without, such as a chat never saved with Autosave chat off.
- mention-stop: telling Coco to stop Maya, such as "Tell Maya to stop.", stops her at once. Her changes stay, and Coco says how far she got, with a Files changed card for what she changed. Mentioning her again continues the same conversation.
- mention-delete-while-working: deleting a teammate from its profile while it works for another chat stops it. The delete dialog says so in an orange line, such as "Maya is working in Coco's chat. Deleting stops it."; Coco then says how far she got.
- mention-remember: a standing rule given through a mention, such as `@Maya from now on, flag video length first.`, is saved to Maya's own memory under the same rules as in her own chat, without a permission card. When her reply comes back, `Maya remembered` or `Maya replaced a rule` shows with the saved sentence under the `Message from` card, as in her own chat; the card's `MEMORY` row opens her `MEMORY.md` at that rule, and the save adds nothing to the Files changed card. Coco saves nothing for a rule meant for another teammate. In `Plan` mode nothing is saved and the reply says so.
- mention-needs-setup: mentioning a teammate whose model can't run on this device starts nothing, and no other model answers as it. Coco says why, such as "Leo runs on OpenCode, which isn't set up on this device", and that another model can be picked in Leo's profile.
- mention-no-self: a teammate never asks itself; its own name is plain text in its chat.
- mention-recall: later, in her own chat, Maya can tell who asked her, in which chat and when, what she was asked and what she answered, as part of her [earlier chats](replies.md). Nothing new shows in Recent chats, the Navigator, View archived or her profile.

## How to get to it

- In the chat input of a teammate's chat or a Copilot chat, type `@` and the teammate's name, or choose `Teammates` in the `@` list and pick the teammate, then type the request and send.

## Driving it

All of these need a Supporter license and at least two teammates in the vault, one of them Maya.

- Ask: in a chat with another teammate, send `@Maya what do you think of this plan? Six features in 90 seconds.` The teammate you are chatting with answers in one line and its reply ends; Send is available again.
- Cards: after sending the mention, the reply shows `Asking` with Maya's face and name, then `Messaged`. Nothing shows under the message that asked, and no clock shows anywhere. When Maya's reply comes back, the new reply starts with `Message from` and her face and name, with none of her words.
- View: while Maya works, choose `Messaged Maya`. The conversation opens read-only, headed by one row with Back, the teammate you are chatting with, a double arrow and Maya; what was sent to her shows as a user's message, then her steps as they come in; no chat input shows. Choose Back: the chat returns. Choose `Message from Maya` once she replied: the same conversation opens with her reply in view. After a stop, send `Send Maya back to it.`, then open it again: a `Continued at <time>` divider separates her earlier work from the new work.
- Several teammates: send `@Maya @Leo does the $8k launch budget hold up?`. `Messaged Maya` and `Messaged Leo` show as two cards. The first reply back is passed on while the running line still names the other, and the reply says the other is still working; the second follows in a reply of its own.
- Keep chatting: while Maya works, send an unrelated question. It gets its own answer.
- Reply back: when Maya is done, a new reply at the end of the chat shows `Message from` with Maya's face and name, then the teammate you are chatting with brings back her view. If she disagrees with the plan, the reply says so plainly.
- Files changed: in `Auto` mode, send `@Maya add one line to <note>`. When her reply comes back, it shows Files changed with that note; its row opens the diff.
- Continue: after Maya's reply comes back, send `@Maya and the intro?`. Her answer builds on the first exchange.
- From a Copilot chat: in a chat with no teammate, send `@Maya is this too long?`. The same one line, then Maya's reply comes back.
- Permission: in `Safe` mode, send `@Maya add one line to <note>`. `Permission required` shows Maya's face and "Maya wants to run …". Choose `Allow once`: the note changes, and when her reply comes back it shows Files changed with it. Send another and choose `Deny once`: the note stays unchanged. In `Plan` mode the note stays unchanged and no card shows.
- Redirect: while Maya works, send `Tell Maya to leave the intro alone.` The reply says it passed it on, and Maya's reply follows the new instruction.
- Question: give Leo an instruction such as "Before changing any note, ask the user which month.", then send `@Leo reconcile the books`. The reply asks which month. Answer `October.` The reply says it passed the answer on, and Leo's reply follows it.
- Progress: while Maya works, send `How's Maya doing?`. The reply says how far she got without stopping her.
- Didn't finish: while Maya works, end her agent's process. The reply says she didn't finish and why. Send `Send Maya back to it.`; her reply builds on what she had done.
- Resume: after Maya's reply comes back, reload the plugin and open the chat from Recent chats. Choose `Message from Maya`: her reply shows below the note that her steps are no longer available; choose Back. Send `@Maya what did you tell me before?`. She recalls the earlier exchange. Repeat with Autosave chat off in a chat never saved: the same holds, and work stopped before she replied still reads `Maya stopped`.
- Stop: send `@Maya check every claim in <note>, one by one`, then `Tell Maya to stop.` while she works. The reply says she was stopped and how far she got; the note keeps any change she made.
- Delete while working: send `@Maya …` with a long job, open Maya's profile, and choose Delete. The dialog shows `Maya is working in <teammate>'s chat. Deleting stops it.` in orange. After Delete, the reply says Maya was deleted and how far she got.
- Remember: in `Safe` mode, send `@Maya from now on, flag video length first.` No permission card shows; when her reply comes back it shows `Maya remembered` with the sentence under `Message from Maya`, and her `MEMORY.md` has the rule with a dated context line. The `MEMORY.md` of the teammate you are chatting with is unchanged. Click the card, then its `MEMORY` row: Maya's `MEMORY.md` opens at that rule. Reload the plugin and reopen the chat: the card is still there. Send `@Maya actually, flag the budget first instead.`: the card reads `Maya replaced a rule`. In `Plan` mode, send `@Maya from now on, open with the hook.`: nothing is saved and the reply says she can't save a rule in this mode. A new chat with Maya follows the saved rule.
- Needs setup: take Leo's agent off this device, then send `@Leo add up the October books.` from Maya's chat. Maya says Leo can't be asked here and why; no `Messaged Leo` card or reply from Leo follows.
- Itself: in Maya's own chat, send `@Maya hello`. Maya answers directly.
- Recall: after Maya's reply comes back, start a new chat with Maya and send `What have we talked about?`. She says who asked her, in which chat, and her answer; Recent chats and her profile show no new chat.

## Gotchas

- Without a Supporter license, `@<name>` is plain text to the chat.
- The teammate you asked never changes its instruction, whatever the mode. A rule it saves applies from its next chat, as in its own chat; mentioning it again in the same chat continues a conversation that started with its earlier memory.
- Maya's work can be viewed only while Obsidian keeps her chat open: after a restart, or once the chat that asked her was shut down, the conversation says her steps are no longer available, with what she sent back, such as her question and her reply, below the note, until mentioning her again continues her conversation. Work that sent nothing back shows the note alone.
- The teammate you asked works in a chat no list shows: not in the [chat tabs](chat-tabs.md), Recent chats or the [Copilot Navigator](copilot-navigator.md).
- A chat can mention teammates only while another teammate exists: a Copilot chat needs at least one teammate, a teammate's own chat at least one besides itself. On Codex models, a chat can mention teammates only if it was opened while Copilot Teammates was available and another teammate existed: it keeps the tools it started with. On Claude and OpenCode models, an open chat gets the tools as soon as both hold, such as when the first teammate is created, and loses them when either stops holding.
- Closing Obsidian or shutting the chat down stops the teammate you asked; its reply never comes back.
- A teammate asked while the license was valid keeps working once it is not, and its reply still comes back.
- The chat's note saves a stopped mention as stopped, whether the user stopped it or the teammate was deleted.
- A settings change that restarts the agent behind the chat or behind the teammate you asked waits until Maya's reply ends. While Maya waits for the user's answer, the restart goes ahead and stops her.
- Stopping the chat's own reply doesn't drop a reply from Maya that is waiting; it comes back after the stop. Stopping the reply that passes hers on ends it there.
- Maya's permission card stays when the chat's own reply is stopped. Stopping or deleting her, closing the chat, or closing Obsidian takes the card away, and her request counts as turned down.
- On Codex models, `No, and tell Codex what to do differently` ends Maya's work without a reply; the chat says the user turned the request down.
- The chat's note keeps each mention's teammate and how it ended, so it survives a restart. A chat without a note reads the same from the replies passed on in it; where a chat has a note, the note's record is the one shown. A teammate that was still working when Obsidian closed is saved as not finished. In a chat without a note, a mention that passed nothing back before Obsidian closed, or one passed back by a Copilot version that didn't record how it ended, keeps its cards, but they don't open. Mentioning it again continues the conversation only while its agent still keeps it; otherwise a new conversation starts.
- The teammate you asked works in the chat's project folder, in the chat's mode, on its own model.
- When one teammate's name starts another's, such as Maya and Maya Chen, `@Maya Chen` mentions only Maya Chen.
- On Claude, a reply that comes back while the teammate you are chatting with is still answering waits for that answer to end, so several replies come back one at a time, each in its own reply. On Codex and OpenCode models the same holds.
