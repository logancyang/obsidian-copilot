# Copilot Teammates

A teammate is an AI helper you set up for one kind of work, such as running your week, editing in your brand voice, or keeping family plans straight. Each teammate has its own instruction, remembers the rules you give it, and keeps its own chats, so personal and business stay apart.

![Two teammates answer the same question: Nora with family plans, Kai with client work](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/2026-11-copilot-teammates/personal-business-apart-8267aacb.png)

Copilot Teammates is a preview for Supporters and works in Copilot Chat on desktop. Without a Supporter license, **New** on the start screen shows a lock; click it to see plans or enter your license key.

## Make a teammate yourself

1. Open Copilot Chat. On the start screen, click **New** (**+**) next to the faces, or **Create your first teammate**.
2. Fill in **Name**, an optional **Title**, and **What _name_ handles**: in your own words, what the teammate does, where things live in your vault, and how it should talk.
3. Click the face to pick another one, or **Upload image** to use your own.
4. Pick a **Model**, then click **Create**.

![The start screen with New and the Create your first teammate card](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-start-screen-673bbb39.png)

The first time, the dialog is filled in as **Coco**, a chief of staff who knows your whole vault. Change anything you like, or click **Create** as is.

![The New teammate dialog filled in as Coco](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-new-teammate-dialog-3850cfce.png)

## Make a teammate from a chat

You can also describe the teammate in any chat and let Copilot or a teammate write it for you.

1. Send a message such as "I need a writing editor called Maya who cuts AI slop from my posts."
2. A **New teammate draft** card shows the name, title, and instruction. Ask for changes in the chat and the card updates.
3. Click **Review and create…**, check the dialog, pick a face and a model, and click **Create**.

Nothing is created until you click **Create**. **Not now** removes the card.

![Asking for a writing editor in chat, reviewing the draft, and creating Maya](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/2026-11-copilot-teammates/first-teammate-eb8a1e4c.webp)

## Talk to a teammate

On the start screen, click the teammate's face and send a message. You can also run **Start a chat with a teammate** from the command palette.

![The start screen with Maya picked: her face, name, and title above the row of faces](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-pick-a-teammate-85b066e3.png)

The chat shows the teammate's face, name, and title at the top, so you always know who you're talking to. With a teammate picked, **Recent chats** lists only that teammate's chats. A new chat starts with the teammate you used last; click **Copilot** for a chat with no teammate.

![Coco's chat with her name and title at the top](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-chat-header-84f5769c.png)

## Get a second opinion

Type `@` and a teammate's name in any chat to ask that teammate, for example `@Maya does the launch email sound like us?`. Teammates are listed under **Teammates** in the `@` menu.

1. The teammate you're chatting with shows **Messaged** with the other teammate's face, and you can keep chatting while it works.
2. When the reply is ready, **Message from** shows, followed by the reply and your current teammate's own view.
3. Click **Messaged** or **Message from** to read the whole exchange between the two teammates.

![Typing @Maya in Coco's chat, Coco messaging Maya, and Maya's reply coming back](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/ask-teammate-second-opinion-2cc26687.webp)

A teammate answers in its own way. Socrates, for example, asks questions instead of giving a verdict:

![Socrates answers with three questions about a newsletter draft](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/2026-11-copilot-teammates/second-opinion-socrates-8e5c836f.png)

More you can do:

- Ask several teammates at once: `@Maya @Leo does the $8k launch budget hold up?`
- Follow up: mention the same teammate again in the same chat and it picks up where it left off.
- Check in: "How's Maya doing?"
- Change course or stop: "Tell Maya to skip the outro." or "Tell Maya to stop."

## Ask a teammate to remember

Tell the teammate a rule in plain words, such as "Remember this: when you list open work, put an owner next to each item." The chat shows **_name_ remembered** with the rule. Click it to see the whole rule, then click **MEMORY** to open the teammate's memory at that rule.

![Coco remembered, opened to show the rule and the MEMORY row](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-remembered-38e05701.png)

- The teammate follows the rule from its next chat.
- To change a rule, say the new one, such as "Stop with the bullets. Write summaries as short paragraphs." The chat shows **_name_ replaced a rule**.
- You can also give a rule through a mention: `@Maya from now on, flag video length first.`
- In **Plan** mode, teammates don't save rules.

To see or edit every rule, open the teammate's profile and click **Rules _name_ remembers**.

## Update a teammate's instruction

The instruction is the description the teammate reads at the start of every chat. Only you change it.

1. Click the teammate's name at the top of its chat, or the gear on its face on the start screen, to open its profile.
2. Click **What _name_ handles**. The instruction opens as a note.
3. Edit the note.

![Coco's profile with What Coco handles and Rules Coco remembers](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-profile-1e598b14.png)

![Coco's instruction open as a note next to her profile](https://images.brevilabs.com/newsletters/copilot-docs/copilot-teammates/teammates-instruction-file-0e4e3358.png)

New chats use the new instruction; chats already open keep the one they started with. If you ask a teammate to change its own instruction, it replies with an **Open instruction** link instead. Keep the instruction under 4,000 characters; the profile shows it in orange when it's longer than the teammate follows well.

On the profile you can also rename the teammate, change its title, face, and model, start a **New chat with _name_**, or delete it. Deleting archives the teammate's chats; you can find them under **View archived**.
